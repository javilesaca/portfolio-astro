## Context

Gamer Agent comes from a real need: players buy on multiple platforms and lose track of monthly spending. The app unifies expense tracking, budget and game discovery in one place.

## Technical decisions

- **Offline-first**: Room as the local source and Firestore as the cloud backup.
- **RAWG API** for the catalog: 2-column grid pagination with dialog detail.
- **WorkManager** para alertas de presupuesto sin necesidad de abrir la app.
